Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Welcome to Senta. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Senta handles information when you use our free online image tools.

1. Images You Process

Senta's image tools are designed to process images directly in your web browser. Images selected for compression, resizing, conversion, cropping, or rotation are processed locally on your device and are not intentionally uploaded to a Senta server by the website's image-processing tools.

2. Personal Information

Senta does not require you to create an account to use the basic image tools. We do not intentionally collect your name, address, phone number, or other personal information through the image-processing features.

3. Cookies

The Senta website does not intentionally use cookies for the basic image processing features. If third-party services are added to the website in the future, their own privacy and cookie policies may apply.

4. Third-Party Services

Senta may use third-party services in the future for website hosting, analytics, advertising, security, or other website functions. Those services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Senta is a general-purpose website. We do not intentionally request personal information from children through the image-processing tools.

6. Security

Because image processing is performed locally in the browser, your selected images are designed to remain on your device while using the available tools.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website's features or services change. Any updated version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the website owner through the contact information provided on the Senta website.

9. Your Consent

By using Senta, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
