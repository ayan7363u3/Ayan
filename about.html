About Senta

Senta is a free online image tools website designed to make common image tasks simple, fast, and accessible.

You can use Senta to process images directly in your browser without needing to install complicated software.

What can you do with Senta?

🗜️ Image Compression
📐 Image Resizing
🔄 Image Conversion
✂️ Image Cropping
🔃 Image Rotation

Simple & Easy

Senta is designed with a clean and simple interface so that anyone can quickly choose a tool, upload an image, process it, and download the result.

Privacy Focused

Our image-processing tools are designed to process images locally in your browser. This means your selected images are not intentionally uploaded to a Senta server by the image-processing features.

Free to Use

Senta's basic image tools are available to use without requiring an account or payment.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide useful, fast, and easy-to-use image tools that work well on both phones and computers.
